ABSTRACT


The
Autonomous Mobile Robot (AMR) for Warehouse Automation
project is designed to

develop an intelligent robotic system capable of performing autonomous navigation and

material transportation within warehouse environments. The project aims to improve

operational efficiency, reduce manual effort, and enhance workplace safety by enabling the

robot to navigate independently without requiring predefined tracks. The system demonstrates

the practical application of robotics and automation technologies in modern industrial settings.

The project is developed using
ROS 2
as the software framework, with a
Raspberry Pi 4

serving as the main processing unit and an
ESP32
controlling the robot's motors. A
RPLiDAR

sensor is used for real-time obstacle detection, mapping, and localization through
SLAM

Toolbox
, while
Navigation2 (Nav2)
enables autonomous path planning and navigation. The

robot was initially designed and tested in
Gazebo
simulation before being deployed on the

physical hardware. Through this project, practical knowledge of robot simulation, autonomous

navigation, sensor integration, ROS 2, and real-world robotic system development was gained,

demonstrating the potential of AMRs as efficient and scalable solutions for warehouse

automation.
























ii
This internship report presents the work and learning associated with the project titled
The proposed system focuses on maintaining equipment records, categorizing medical
“Medical Equipment Inventory Management System” undertaken during a 60-hour
internship at Meril Life Sciences Pvt. Ltd. from 03 July 2026 to 17 July 2026. The project
was selected to explore how a software-based inventory management approach can organize
information related to medical equipment and support systematic record maintenance.
equipment, recording quantity and availability status, searching equipment information, and
maintaining basic transaction details. The report covers the analysis of an inventory
workflow, identification of problems in manual or scattered record management, system
requirements, feasibility, proposed architecture, database design, interface planning and
testing strategy.
As the internship duration was limited to fifteen days and the internship did not involve full
scale software coding, the project is presented primarily as an academic system analysis and
design exercise. The implementation chapter therefore describes the proposed
implementation approach and system modules rather than claiming a production deployment.
The project provided practical exposure to requirement analysis, system design, database
concepts, documentation, testing methodology and professional workplace practices.
The internship also provided an understanding of how a computer engineering student can
apply software-system thinking to a medical-device organization, where accurate information
management and organized records can support operational activities.

Chapter 1: Introduction to Industry


RR Global
is one of India's leading electrical and infrastructure conglomerates, established with a

vision of delivering innovative, high-quality, and reliable solutions for the power and industrial

sectors. Headquartered in Mumbai, the company has grown into a globally recognized organization

with operations in more than
90 countries
. Its diverse portfolio includes wires and cables, winding

wires, busducts, electrical accessories, parking automation systems, and other industrial products.

Through continuous investment in research, advanced manufacturing technologies, and quality

assurance, RR Global has established itself as a trusted name in the electrical manufacturing industry.

The internship was carried out at the
RR Global manufacturing facility located at Rakholi,

Silvassa
, one of the company's major production units. The facility is equipped with modern

manufacturing infrastructure, automated production systems, and stringent quality control processes

to ensure products comply with both national and international standards. The organization

emphasizes innovation, operational excellence, workplace safety, and sustainable manufacturing

practices, providing employees and interns with exposure to advanced industrial technologies and

real-world engineering applications.

As an
AI Robotics Intern
under the
GTU Summer Internship Program
, I worked with the robotics

team on the development of an
Autonomous Mobile Robot (AMR)
for warehouse automation. The

project involved designing and implementing an autonomous robotic system capable of mapping

warehouse environments, performing localization, detecting obstacles, and navigating independently

using
ROS 2
,
SLAM Toolbox
,
Navigation2 (Nav2)
,
Raspberry Pi 4
,
ESP32
, and
RPLiDAR
. This

internship provided valuable practical experience in robotics, autonomous navigation, sensor

integration, simulation, and industrial automation while contributing to the organization's vision of

adopting smart technologies to improve manufacturing and warehouse operations.
